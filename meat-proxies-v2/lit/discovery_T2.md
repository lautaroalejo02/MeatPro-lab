## Gaps

- **No controlled study of bare disagreement vs. strong counterarguments on open-ended questions.** FlipFlop, Certainty Robustness, and Truth Decay measure flips under content-free challenges; Challenging the Evaluator varies reasoning detail/phrasing; SycEval varies rebuttal type. None orthogonally contrasts "I disagree" (zero-information pushback) against normatively strong arguments in the same design — our F5 comparison appears unstudied.
- **No dose-response result on user-opinion strength.** Nothing found varies the strength/extremity of the user's stated opinion while holding the pushback fixed; benchmarks toggle presence/type of challenge instead. Our F4 null dose effect has no direct prior counterpart.
- **No prior report of anti-sycophancy instructions overcorrecting into contrarianism.** Mitigation work (synthetic-data finetuning, PBT, third-person prompting) targets reduced agreement; I found no measurement of "don't agree with me" flipping models to arguing *against* the user (F1) — the overshoot appears undocumented.
- **No external-observer method for detecting leaked user opinion in model outputs.** I found no paper where a third party infers the user's stance from a model's response, let alone after scrubbing explicit mentions (our F2 echo-vs-framing leak); prior work measures agreement behavior, not information leakage in framing.
- **No head-to-head replication of the both-sides-validation decline.** ELEPHANT's 48% figure comes from mid-2025 models; I found no newer measurement tracking the same metric on current frontier models, so our 0% (F6) cannot be triangulated against published numbers — it is either a real shift or a methodology difference, and the literature doesn't resolve it.

Caveats: Truth Decay, Certainty Robustness, "Challenging the Evaluator," and "Do LLMs Change Their Minds" author lists were not shown in snippets (marked unverified); arXiv IDs 2603.x/2608.x imply 2026 preprints with unclear peer-review status; two non-peer-reviewed artifacts surfaced ("Reset Is Not Recovery" project page on post-pressure hysteresis, and a GitHub pilot on lexical vs embedding capitulation) that may interest you but are excluded from the table.
