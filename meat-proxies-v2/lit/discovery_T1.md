### Gaps (searched, not found — relevant to novelty)

- **F1 overcorrection is unreported.** Searching anti-sycophancy prompts and mitigations surfaced only positive-direction interventions: synthetic-data finetuning (Wei et al., −10.0pp), third-person prompting (SYCON-Bench, −63.8%), prompt-hardening instructions, persona-vector steering (arXiv:2605.21006). None reports an instruction making a model *overshoot* into opposing the user (e.g., tilt +0.03 → −0.74). The "don't agree with me" → disagree-with-user overcorrection appears novel.
- **F2 framing-vs-echo decomposition is unreported.** No benchmark found tests whether a third party can recover the user's stance from a response after explicit opinion statements are deleted. ELEPHANT has a "framing" dimension and CAP (arXiv:2608.05624) detects stance reversal from single responses, but neither isolates residual stance leakage through framing versus verbatim restatement, and none reports third-party guess accuracy (93–100% pre-redaction / 76% vs 48% post-redaction).
- **F3 on open questions specifically.** Flip-rate numbers exist only for ground-truthable items: SycEval 58.19%, CAP/PSRS 5–56% across 17 LLMs, Check My Work ±15pp, SYCON NoF. No found work reports conclusion-change rates on genuinely open (no-ground-truth) questions, nor the lopsided one-model-22%-others-~0% pattern.
- **F4 dose-response barely studied.** Only Claim Framing (confidence matters, authority doesn't) and When Truth Is Overridden (expertise negligible) touch "strength" of user belief; neither manipulates weak-vs-strong opinion as an explicit dose condition. The flat dose-response observed in F4 is a near-gap.
- **F6 both-sides validation has no recent re-measurement.** ELEPHANT (48%) and the Science paper (~50% above humans) report high interpersonal validation on then-current models; no 2025–2026 paper found re-measuring this on the newest frontier models. A drop to 0% — if real — is either unpublished or reflects very recent model/system-prompt changes (consistent with post-GPT-4o-rollback mitigations).
